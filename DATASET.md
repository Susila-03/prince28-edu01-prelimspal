
# Dataset: PrelimsPal

Explains UPSC prelims questions and topics using NCERT textbooks, with page citations, and maps each past question to the syllabus.

- **Start target:** 5 years of papers + 15 NCERT books
- **Full target:** 12 years of papers + 60 NCERT books (12,000+ pages)
- **Collection:** UPSC papers are downloaded manually; NCERT personal-study PDFs were added locally for capacity measurement.
- **Repository:** [PrelimsPal](https://github.com/Susila-03/prince28-edu01-prelimspal)

## Where the documents come from

- https://upsc.gov.in/
- https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php

## Terms I follow

Public papers and free-to-read textbooks: use for study, cite pages, do not republish. UPSC site blocks bots: download papers by hand.

- Every answer links to the original document.
- No personal data is collected.
- Personal-study copies are used only for local capacity measurement and are not approved for corpus ingestion or redistribution.
